# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how our video publishing application (the “Service”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and processes information when you use the Service or connect third-party platforms such as TikTok.

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect and process the following categories of information.

### Account Information

When you create or use an account with the Service, we may process information necessary to identify and authenticate your account.

### TikTok Account Information

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization process, we may receive information authorized by you, which may include:

* your TikTok user identifier;
* basic profile information;
* username or display name;
* profile image;
* permissions and scopes granted to the Service;
* account capabilities required for content publishing.

The specific information available to us depends on the permissions you approve and the functionality provided by TikTok's APIs.

### Authorization Tokens

When you authorize the TikTok integration, the Service may receive access tokens and refresh tokens.

These credentials are used to communicate securely with TikTok's APIs on your behalf.

Authorization tokens are not intended to be publicly exposed and are stored using security measures designed to protect them from unauthorized access.

### Content and Publication Information

When you use the Service to publish content, we may process:

* video files;
* captions and titles;
* privacy settings;
* publication preferences;
* content disclosure information, including whether content is identified as AI-generated where applicable;
* publication identifiers;
* publication status;
* errors associated with publication attempts;
* timestamps and related technical metadata.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information collected through the Service to:

* provide and operate the Service;
* authenticate users;
* connect authorized third-party accounts;
* communicate with TikTok's official APIs;
* submit videos for publication when requested by the user;
* retrieve publication status;
* maintain publication history;
* manage and refresh authorization credentials;
* allow users to disconnect integrations;
* diagnose errors and technical problems;
* protect the security and integrity of the Service;
* comply with applicable legal obligations and platform requirements.

We do not use your TikTok authorization credentials to perform actions outside the permissions you have granted.

## 3. TikTok Integration

The Service integrates with TikTok using TikTok's official APIs and authorization mechanisms.

When you connect your TikTok account, you are redirected to TikTok to review and approve the requested permissions.

We do not ask you to provide your TikTok password directly to the Service.

Information received from TikTok is used only as necessary to provide the features you request and operate the integration.

Your use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

## 4. Content Publishing

When you request that content be published to TikTok, the Service may transmit your video, caption, publication settings, and other required information to TikTok.

The Service does not guarantee that submitted content will be accepted, published, distributed, or retained by TikTok.

TikTok may independently review, restrict, reject, remove, or otherwise process content according to its own policies.

## 5. AI-Generated Content

The Service may support content generated or modified using artificial intelligence.

Where applicable, information indicating that content is AI-generated or AI-modified may be processed and transmitted to supported third-party platforms to comply with their disclosure requirements.

Users remain responsible for ensuring that their content complies with applicable platform rules and legal requirements.

## 6. How We Store and Protect Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Sensitive authorization credentials, such as access tokens and refresh tokens, are protected using appropriate security measures.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or transmit information when necessary:

* with TikTok or another third-party platform that you explicitly connect to the Service;
* with infrastructure or hosting providers necessary to operate the Service;
* when required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental request;
* to investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity;
* to protect our rights, users, or the security of the Service.

Third-party providers are only provided information reasonably necessary for their applicable function.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Service may rely on third-party services for functionality such as hosting, authentication, data storage, or content delivery.

These providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party platforms connected by users operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Publication history may be retained after a social media account is disconnected where necessary to preserve the user's publication records.

Authorization credentials that are no longer necessary may be deleted, invalidated, or revoked as appropriate.

## 10. Disconnecting Your TikTok Account

You may disconnect your TikTok account from the Service.

When an account is disconnected, the Service will stop using the associated authorization credentials for future TikTok operations.

Where supported, authorization credentials may also be revoked through TikTok's official API.

Disconnecting TikTok from the Service does not automatically delete videos or other content previously published to TikTok.

## 11. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to information that we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

Where applicable, deleting an account may include removal of:

* stored TikTok account identifiers;
* authorization credentials;
* account-related configuration;
* publication information associated with the user.

Content already published to a third-party platform may need to be deleted separately through that platform.

## 12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

* request access to personal information we maintain about you;
* request correction of inaccurate information;
* request deletion of your information;
* withdraw authorization for connected third-party services;
* object to or restrict certain processing activities where applicable.

You may disconnect connected accounts at any time through available Service functionality.

## 13. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the Service or the connected third-party platforms.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 14. International Data Processing

Depending on the infrastructure used to operate the Service and the third-party platforms you connect, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

## 15. Security Incidents

If we become aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will take reasonable steps to investigate, mitigate, and respond to the incident in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## 16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service develops, legal requirements change, or third-party integrations are modified.

When changes are made, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document will be updated.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## 17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, requests concerning your personal information, or requests for account or data deletion, please contact us using the contact information provided on the Service's official website.
